## 1. Target keywords (priority order)

Priority reflects audience tiers requested: US first, then UK/CA/AU, then Indonesia/Philippines (English-only), then rest of world. All keywords below are in English since the site is English-only; no localized variants are proposed.

1. **bulk image resizer** (exact brand/category match — primary head term, US/global)
2. **resize multiple images at once** (US, high intent, matches hero copy almost verbatim)
3. **batch image resizer chrome extension** (US/Tier-1, captures "chrome extension" + "batch" searchers)
4. **free bulk image resizer** (US/Tier-1 — "free" is a real, provable claim on the page)
5. **resize images in browser** (US/Tier-1/Indonesia-Philippines — emphasizes local/privacy angle, no upload)
6. **resize and convert images in bulk** (Tier-1 — captures the format-conversion feature, a real differentiator)
7. **batch resize images for social media** (global tail — ties to the "Smart Presets" feature, genuinely implied by page content)

Notes:
- Avoided invented/location-specific terms (e.g. no "resize images Indonesia") since the product and page have no localized content to back them.
- Dropped "bulk photo resizer online" (previously #6) per human review — misleading, since processing is local/in-browser, not server-based, and "online" implies upload.

Sellers on Etsy/Shopify/Amazon routinely need consistent product-image dimensions and formats; the page already supports folder-batch resize + format conversion, so this section would just reframe existing features for that audience (no new functionality claims).

Expand on the already-stated fact that no files are uploaded, contrasting with typical web-based resize tools that require file upload — useful for privacy-conscious users and for differentiating from upload-based competitors, without claiming anything not already on the page.
