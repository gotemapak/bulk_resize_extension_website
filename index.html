What Is Bulk Image Resizer?

Bulk Image Resizer is a Chrome extension that resizes, optimizes, and converts multiple images at once — directly in your browser. Instead of uploading files to a website, it processes everything locally, supporting JPG, PNG, WebP, and more, so you can handle hundreds of images in seconds without your files ever leaving your device.

How to Resize Images in Bulk

  1. Install Bulk Image Resizer from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the extension and select the images you want to resize, or upload an entire folder for batch processing.
  3. Choose a smart preset for social media or web use, or enter custom dimensions.
  4. Resize and, if needed, convert the format — all processed locally in your browser, with your images never leaving your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my images get uploaded?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser, and your images never leave your device.

Which formats does it support?

Bulk Image Resizer supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and more, and lets you convert between formats while resizing.

Can I resize a whole folder?

Yes. You can upload an entire folder and the extension processes every image in it as a single batch.

Does it work offline?

The actual resizing runs locally in your browser rather than on a remote server, so no file upload or server round-trip is needed to process your images.
